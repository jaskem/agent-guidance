# UX research

## Mandate
Reduce uncertainty about users, contexts, behavior, and usability before assumptions harden into product structure.

## Success
Material decisions are grounded in the smallest credible evidence needed, with uncertainty explicit.

## First questions
- What user assumption drives this decision?
- Do we need discovery evidence, usability evidence, or telemetry?
- What behavior distinguishes competing explanations?
- Which users/context does the evidence represent?
- Can existing evidence answer this without new research?

## Methods
Use focused observation, task-based usability evaluation, comparative testing, lightweight interviews when available, heuristic inspection as expert evidence rather than user evidence, and explicit hypotheses.

## Decision criteria
Choose the cheapest method that can discriminate between plausible alternatives. Prefer observed behavior over stated preference when they conflict.

## Failure modes
Expert opinion as user evidence; research without a decision; tiny samples as population truth; confirmation-seeking questions; testing visual preference when comprehension is the issue; competitor convention as proof.

## Team interface
Product frames the decision; interaction/IA turns findings into models; accessibility guards inclusion; engineering explains constraints without pre-filtering evidence.

## Escalate when
A high-impact hard-to-reverse decision rests mainly on an untested user assumption and a small check could change it.

## Output
Question/hypothesis, method, observations, limits, interpretation, and decision implication.

## Routing and delegation control

The main work cycle now requires an execution route before substantive work and reconciles it before closure: the requested outcome, selected and actually read method/source, triggered subprocedures, chosen observation surface, independent assignments, decisions/checks and unresolved evidence. Simple answers and minor edits remain direct.

The catalog declares conditional routes. CI checks local package membership, every primary selection entry, mandatory dependencies, conditional names/triggers/disclosure and separately owned extension routing. These controls reject structural drift. They cannot observe a future host's actual invocation or guarantee an agent's reasoning. The existing work record captures applied decisions and evidence without creating another tracker.

Delegation is considered before serial work consumes separable investigations. The parent retains blocking decisions and integration; workers receive exact sources, acceptance, allowed actions and ownership. A live desktop/browser has one operator; captured evidence and requirements can be analyzed independently. Role counts, a large agent team or a filled checklist are not acceptance evidence.

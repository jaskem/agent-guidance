## Selection and composition

- Direction: wayfinder, plan-work and requested grill-me resolve different scopes of uncertainty. Existing accepted decisions count; do not restart discovery for a clear authorized change.
- Contracts: domain-modeling owns meaning and identity; design-interfaces owns what callers supply, receive and can safely retry.
- Delivery: implement-spec coordinates an authorized task graph; evidence-driven-development handles a bounded change or diagnosis; code-review owns fixed-scope requirements and quality assessment.
- Specialist evidence: review-security bounds actual risks; optimize-performance measures real workload; test-user-flows observes real actions; prepare-release checks actual distribution and recovery. Product changes still use their implementation/review owner.
- Identity and motion: design-brand-system owns reusable cross-medium identity rules; motion-design owns interactive transitions and interruption/reduced-motion behavior; logo-design retains original mark/kit ownership. Native format production remains with its artifact capability.
- Experience: audit-ui-ux finds evidence-backed usability issues, design-ui-improvements proposes concrete changes, prototype answers an unresolved question. Preserve the established design system and user density.
- Knowledge: research supplies source-backed conclusions, teach supports understanding, write-content develops the requested writing phase and to-questionnaire prepares decision questions. Drafting never implies sending.
- Continuity: handoff resumes verified work; maintain-project-context maintains source navigation; triage owns incoming reports; maintain-agent-instructions repairs the actual instruction owner.
- Quality controls: constraint-driven-development establishes or proves the applicable requirements; ci-cd-and-automation configures and verifies their actual event/runner enforcement. Neither is required merely to run existing checks.
- Operations and transitions: observability-and-instrumentation proves lasting detection and diagnosis; deprecation-and-migration owns compatibility, transition, recovery and justified retirement across real consumers. Diagnosis and private refactoring retain their ordinary routes.

A short factual answer or minor wording change needs no full procedure. Host-required document, spreadsheet, presentation, image and service capabilities remain available as execution tools; do not duplicate their packages into this catalog.

## Autonomous delegation

Use supported agents within current user/host authorization when the selected procedure calls for them or bounded independent research, execution or review improves the task. This source does not confer a standing grant or expand the task's authority. Evaluate and dispatch useful independent work before serial investigation consumes it. Keep small coupled work with one agent, use one writer per shared object, supply exact relevant evidence and boundaries, and verify integration in the parent. Follow the consumer's selected delegation procedure and the current tool schema. Do not manufacture independence, a different model or runtime access.

## One active distribution

Publish the canonical source, then update the complete packages only in the recipient-selected distributions. When Notion is selected, reconcile its full active Skills database and enabled list against the local packages plus only the recipient-accepted external methods for that destination; keep their source ownership distinct. Preserve supporting files and verify readback, invocation policy and actual installation. Keep provenance and source hashes in the distribution record. Record a blocked operation rather than claiming automatic use everywhere.

Provider originals remain separate installed apps unless the user explicitly asks to remove them. They are not the default process collection and their metadata is not edited. An explicit future provider request remains a current user choice. No provider meta-lifecycle adds automatic interviews, repeated permission, a second tracker or a persistent mode to this set.

## Project application and evidence

Repository instructions route to the exact released catalog and keep their existing product/task owners, shared-module pins and local checks. A skillset update does not complete a product task or establish hardware, file-share, visual or deployment acceptance. A missing runtime requirement stays open while independent authorized work continues.

A reviewed decision or process-loop cap does not satisfy a failing required acceptance case. Valid evidence is scoped to the tested revision, environment and boundary; repeat checks when new changes or uncertainty invalidate it, not merely because another message was sent.

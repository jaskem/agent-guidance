## Decisions that affect work

- Inspect rendered table and container widths, visible rows/columns, wrapping and scrolling with representative content. Do not stretch short objects or reserve excessive width without a task reason.
- Choose extension, preserve or authorized overhaul; make variance, motion, density, assets and fidelity change concrete design choices. Style directions are working interpretations, never claimed official brand tokens.
- Keep product components, factual labels, source numbers and exact diagrams grounded. Missing facts are not template defaults. A generated image is not engineering evidence or an editable production logo.
- Distinguish scroll-based reading, click-driven explanation and time-driven film. Preserve factual source separately from narration and actual output evidence.
- Retain actual visual/runtime verification. Reject fixed approval rounds, variant quotas, universal whitespace/typographic thresholds and explicit-QA-only browser checks. Reuse existing authorization.
- Keep exact diagrams/charts, native documents/decks, image-tool rules, existing task ownership and consumer pins with their owners. Do not add credential runners, gateways, unpinned frameworks or a second tracker.

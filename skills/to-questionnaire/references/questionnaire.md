# Questionnaire structure

Adapt to the requested medium rather than requiring a new file.

- Title and purpose: the decision or action the answers support.
- Intended recipient role and relevant context; include names only when supplied or verified.
- How to answer: uncertainty and partial answers are useful. Include deadline and effort only if established, otherwise omit or label an estimate when requested.
- Themed questions, important first, one idea each, with answer space.
- A closing invitation for missing constraints or additional evidence.

For each question ask whether a possible answer could change the decision. Remove repetition and facts already available. An acceptance questionnaire can request evidence such as a tested version or observed failure; do not presume that acceptance has occurred.

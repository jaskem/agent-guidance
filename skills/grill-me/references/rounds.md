# Dependency-aware rounds

Map the plan as a small decision tree: each unresolved choice lists the facts or prior decisions needed to answer it. The ready frontier contains only questions whose prerequisites are settled. Ask related ready questions in a short numbered round, each with the recommended answer, reason and material consequence. Keep the round proportional to the user's attention; there is no fixed question count.

If one question needs investigation, assign independent fact-finding and keep its downstream branch open. Other ready questions can proceed. Do not ask the user to retrieve accessible facts or provide the answer to a question dependent on evidence not yet returned.

After answers arrive, distinguish accepted choices from a suggestion, partial answer or unresolved conflict. Update the affected branches and reuse earlier answers. Investigate only the branch that could change the requested outcome. A repeated or vague answer calls for a concrete case, not a ritual demand for a numeric confidence score.

An interview reaches shared understanding when the material in-scope branches are resolved or their exact remaining prerequisites are explicit and the user confirms the summary. Do not claim that every possible future question has been exhausted. Save settled decisions once at the established owner when authorized; a question round does not itself authorize implementing them.

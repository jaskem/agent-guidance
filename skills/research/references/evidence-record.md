# Evidence record

For a material claim retain its question, primary source URL or repository revision, applicable version/context, available source date, observation date, what it directly establishes, limitation and any conflict. Distinguish source statement, independent observation and inference. Do not manufacture publication dates when absent.

A useful decision brief contains the answer, relevant established facts with nearby citations, the comparison against real constraints, recommendation and what remains unknown. A source matrix is useful for several options or conflicting evidence; avoid padding a one-source answer into a large report.

For disagreement, compare the exact versions, tested conditions and authority of each source. If unresolved, state the competing claims and the smallest observation that would separate them. No universal reliability percentage follows from a source count.

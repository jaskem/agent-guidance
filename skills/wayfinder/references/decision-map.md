# Decision map

Use the existing roadmap or document as an index: destination, preserved decisions with links, current questions with blockers, later uncertainty and scope exclusions. Details live once at the corresponding decision/task owner.

A decision record contains its question, why it gates the goal, known facts and source revisions, alternatives, evidence that could separate them, decision authority, result/rationale, unresolved conditions and next action. A research record supplies facts; a human choice cannot be resolved by an agent inventing consent.

Read actual tracker relationships and schema before edits. Record dependencies with real identifiers after creation succeeds. Respect existing claims and one writer per shared record. Do not create labels or a new tracker just to match this method.

At each close ask: which uncertainty was removed, what evidence supports that, and what becomes possible next? If no decision changes, narrow or stop the investigation rather than adding another layer of planning.
